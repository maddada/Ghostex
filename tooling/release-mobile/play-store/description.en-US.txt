Ghostex puts the coding agents running on your computer in your pocket. Watch them work, answer their questions, approve their next step and start new work, wherever you are.

This app is the companion to Ghostex, the free desktop workspace for command-line coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Cursor. It connects to Ghostex on your own Mac, Windows or Linux computer, so install Ghostex there first: ghostex.dev.

WATCH AND REPLY TO SESSIONS
• Every agent session on every connected computer, grouped by project, with live status: working, done, or waiting for you.
• A chat view of each conversation built for the phone: messages, tool calls, edited files and plans, easy to follow.
• Answer an agent's questions and approve its next step right in the chat.
• Send follow-ups, dictate or talk with the voice agent, and attach photos, screenshots and files.
• Search the open and closed sessions on all your computers.
• Share one chat through a link. The other person follows it in a browser, and you decide whether their messages are held, sent or view-only.
• An attention list shows what is waiting for you.

OPEN A REAL TERMINAL
• Attach to any session's terminal, drawn with Termux terminal components.
• Extra keys for Esc, Ctrl, Tab, arrows and more, laid out for a phone.
• Pinch to zoom, select and copy, and paste safely. External keyboards work too.

START NEW WORK
• Start new agent sessions, add projects and clone repositories without walking back to your desk.
• Find any prompt you sent before and send it again.
• Autopilot can keep sessions moving while you are away. You choose what it is allowed to do.

SEE WHAT YOUR AGENTS BUILT
• Web Preview opens the dev servers running on your computer, such as localhost:3000, right on the phone.
• Read your project's Markdown and HTML docs, plans and notes, and browse the project's files.
• Open a project's changes.

CONNECT IN ABOUT A MINUTE
• Easy Connect: open Mobile & Remote in Ghostex on your computer and scan the code it shows. Nothing else to install.
• Or connect with Tailscale or SSH using an address, username and password.
• Add several computers: work, home, a build server.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Free. No account to create, no ads and no in-app purchases.
• Your code and conversations travel encrypted between your phone and your own computer. The agents run there, under your own accounts.
• Your code, prompts and conversations are never sent to us.

Ghostex is open source. Questions, ideas and bug reports are welcome on GitHub and Discord, linked from ghostex.dev.
