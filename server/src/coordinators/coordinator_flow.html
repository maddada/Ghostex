Ghostex coordinator · how it works

How working with a coordinator goes

One coordinator per project. You ask and pick; it plans, starts the threads, follows them and brings back what needs you.

You prompt and pick It runs the threads Far fewer threads to watch

The flow

  1. Ask for what you want, in plain words. I want to add feature X, show me UX options in a Live page. or Triage all my issues in a Live page.
  2. A page lands in your Inbox, right above the chat box: designs or choices, drawn so you can see them.
  3. Pick and comment on the page, then send it to the coordinator from the end of the page.
  4. It starts the threads that do the work, with the agents you said to use for each kind of work (kept in its memory).
  5. It comes back only when it needs you: a Needs you in the sidebar and the Inbox, nothing else to watch.

Why it beats managing threads by hand

No more babysitting threads

No looking for, snoozing or settling threads. It happens by itself, so the sidebar stays nearly empty and your head stays clear.

You prompt and pick

The coordinator manages all the work. You decide where it matters and skip the rest.

You in the loop, not buried

A Needs you in the sidebar when it matters. Jump in when you want, pick or snooze, tell it to cut a release once you are done testing, or go play a game.

Uses what you have

It spreads work over your computers by the resources they have free, and over your agents by the usage they have left.

Gets better with time

It saves how you like to work in this project. New coordinators can copy memories from others. Better than AGENTS.md, since its memory does not change how worker threads behave.

Better prompts than yours

Once it knows the product it rewords every ask into a full brief for the thread, with the files, the limits and the proof to bring back.

Always free to take more

It stays idle after handing out the work, so you can send it more asks at any time, however unrelated.

Pages it makes for you

Designs to choose from, plans to compare, lists to triage. Each one reads well on a phone too.

From your phone
Ask for Live pages. Use them to get information back and to make decisions. Reading a long chat reply is a much worse experience.
